## The Schrödinger Equation

The time-dependent Schrödinger equation governs the evolution of a quantum state:

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{H} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)$$

where $\hat{H}$ is the Hamiltonian operator, $\Psi$ is the wave function,
and $\hbar$ is the reduced Planck constant.

## Einstein's Mass-Energy Equivalence

Perhaps the most famous equation in physics is $E = mc^2$, relating
energy $E$ to mass $m$ through the speed of light $c$.

## Euler's Identity

A beautiful connection between fundamental constants:

$$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$

## Dirac Notation

In quantum mechanics, we often use bra-ket notation. The probability
of measuring state $|\psi\rangle$ in state $|\phi\rangle$ is:

$$P = |\langle \phi | \psi \rangle|^2$$
